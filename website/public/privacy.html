Your verification and permissions.

Verification uses Discord OAuth to identify you and add you to the server shown before approval. We store your Discord ID, username, joined server and verification time.

Future server moves are optional. If you check the permission box, we encrypt and retain your Discord access and refresh tokens while the 180-day permission is active. Expired encrypted tokens are deleted by daily cleanup. The community owner can use this permission to add you to another server they manage where the verification bot is installed. Tokens are refreshed only when needed and are never shown to the owner.

To revoke future moves, remove this application in Discord’s Authorized Apps settings. Revocation prevents future additions; it does not remove you from servers already joined.

Without this permission, tokens are discarded after verification. Existing verification records cannot be used for automatic moves until you verify again and opt in. Your Discord password is never shared with us. Contact the community owner to request deletion of your verification record.
