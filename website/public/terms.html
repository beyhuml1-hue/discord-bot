Terms and conditions

By checking the future server additions checkbox and completing Discord authorization, you permit verification into the displayed server and future additions to other servers managed by this community’s owner, without a new prompt for each move.

Verification records have no scheduled expiry.

Future server moves

Newly approved permission has no scheduled expiry and remains active until you revoke access or Discord invalidates the authorization. Previously granted permissions retain their original expiry unless you approve again. The owner can add you only to a server where the verification bot is installed. Discord may require you to accept that server’s rules. Server bans and Discord limits still apply.

Stored authorization

We encrypt and store your Discord access and refresh tokens for future additions. The owner cannot view them. We refresh tokens when needed and delete expired legacy permissions during daily cleanup. We also store your Discord ID, username and verification history.

Your choice and revocation

The checkbox is optional and starts unchecked. You can verify into the displayed server without permitting future moves. Revoke future moves by removing this app in Discord’s Authorized Apps settings. Revocation stops future additions; it does not remove you from servers already joined.

We never receive your Discord password. Contact the community owner to request deletion of your verification records.
