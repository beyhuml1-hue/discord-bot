Terms and conditions

By checking “Accept terms and conditions” and completing Discord authorization, you permit verification into the displayed server and future additions to other servers managed by this community’s owner, without a new prompt for each move.

Verification lasts 180 days for each server. Repeat attempts during that period show “Already verified.” You may verify again when it expires.

Future server moves

This permission lasts 180 days. The owner can add you only to a server where the verification bot is installed. Discord may require you to accept that server’s rules. Server bans and Discord limits still apply.

Stored authorization

We encrypt and store your Discord access and refresh tokens for future additions. The owner cannot view them. We refresh tokens when needed and delete expired permissions during daily cleanup. We also store your Discord ID, username and verification history.

Your choice and revocation

The checkbox is optional and starts unchecked. You can verify into the displayed server without permitting future moves. Revoke future moves by removing this app in Discord’s Authorized Apps settings. Revocation stops future additions; it does not remove you from servers already joined.

We never receive your Discord password. Contact the community owner to request deletion of your verification records.
